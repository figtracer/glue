# On-demand refuel

pay less to get started.

Get gas for a wallet with one request. Pay from Tempo mainnet with pathUSD or USDC.e and receive native gas on the destination chain.

## Use it

**In a browser:** open [glue.figtracer.com](https://glue.figtracer.com), choose the token, amount and destination, review the quote and pay. The website also offers Sepolia routes for testnet ETH.

**With an agent:** give it [Glue's llms.txt](https://glue.figtracer.com/llms.txt). That is the source for supported MPP requests, payment handling and delivery tracking.

This service runs through the website and API. No local scheduler is installed. To keep a wallet funded automatically, enable [gas maintenance](gas.md).
